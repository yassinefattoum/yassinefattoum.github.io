01 / Profile

Software engineering for real-world systems.

WHO I AM

I am a Software Engineer with three years of experience developing production-ready software, including industrial machine-vision systems for automotive and textile applications.

My expertise covers C++, C#, OpenCV, reusable software libraries, application development, system integration and AI-based solutions. I also have experience integrating software with industrial cameras, PLCs, databases and production systems.

Beyond implementation, I place strong emphasis on software architecture and project structure. I aim to design modular, well-organised and maintainable software, with clear separation of responsibilities, reusable components and a structure that supports future development. I believe that good software is not only about making features work, but also about building a solid foundation that makes systems easier to understand, extend, test and maintain.

My experience spans the full software development lifecycle, from feasibility studies and architectural design to implementation, performance optimisation, system integration and on-site validation. Based in Karlsruhe, Germany, I am open to opportunities in software engineering, software design, computer vision and industrial automation.
